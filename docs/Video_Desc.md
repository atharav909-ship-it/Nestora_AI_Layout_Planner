# Nestora — Video Demonstration

This video demonstrates the working prototype of **Nestora — AI Bathroom Studio**, an AI-assisted bathroom planning system combining design intelligence with deterministic spatial validation.

## Demo Video

🎥 [Watch the Nestora Prototype Demonstration] (https://youtu.be/BGwDze1LE70)
### Demonstrated Features

- Room dimensions and design-intelligence inputs
- AI-assisted bathroom planning
- Multiple layout generation strategies
- Spatially validated fixture placement
- Interactive 2D floor plan
- Synchronized 3D bathroom visualization
- Product and budget planning
- AI design review and recommendations
- Saved designs and PDF report generation

> **Nestora principle:** AI reasons. The spatial engine validates.
